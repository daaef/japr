Clinical Trials and Public Health Policy in European Healthcare Systems

AU

Author User

European Health Policy Institute

Published Sep 12, 2026 · Language: English

Abstract

This study examines the intersection of clinical trial frameworks and public health policy across European healthcare systems, identifying best practices for equitable access. We compare regulatory environments, reimbursement policies, and patient-outcome reporting in twelve EU member states and propose a harmonized transparency standard.
